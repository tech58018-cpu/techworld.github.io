2. Information We and Our Service Providers Collect

Information you give us: Video Downloader & Vault does not ask for personal details. In Meri Bhakti App, if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique Google account ID from Google. We never receive your Google password. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically by third-party services (both apps): When you use the apps, the analytics, crash-reporting, messaging and advertising services listed in section 5 may collect:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and an app instance ID
  • App activity, such as screens viewed, features used and ad interactions
  • App info and performance data, such as crash logs and diagnostics
  • Your IP address, which these services process to deliver and measure ads and to keep the apps working

Video Downloader & Vault: Videos you download, files in the vault, your download list and in-app browser data are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.

Meri Bhakti App: Your Jaap counter progress and app settings are stored on your device. The app loads devotional content such as audio, wallpapers and horoscope text over the internet, so the servers that host this content receive technical request data such as your IP address.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Content and settings on your device (downloaded videos, vault files, Jaap progress, saved wallpapers) stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s own data from your device.
  • Video Downloader & Vault has no accounts. In Meri Bhakti App, your account information (name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID) is kept until you delete your account. See section 7.
  • Data held by the service providers in section 5 is kept according to their own policies. You can reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, such as emails you sent us, write to bigdealzapps@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Delete Your Meri Bhakti Account and Data

You can delete your Meri Bhakti App account and the data linked to it at any time, inside the app.

  1. Open Meri Bhakti App and go to Settings.
  2. Tap “Delete Account”.
  3. Confirm when asked.

    4. Your account and the data linked to it (name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID) are permanently deleted.

Data stored only on your device, such as Jaap progress and settings, is removed when you uninstall the app. Data held by advertising and analytics providers is handled under their own policies (see section 5).

8. Data Security

Data sent between the apps and the services they use is encrypted in transit where the service supports it. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Content you save stays on your device, so keeping the device itself secure also protects it.

The apps are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13, or under the higher age that applies in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
